From image to useful guidance

A clear five-step path to a better-informed choice.

Smart Waste separates what the image suggests from the rules that shape a disposal recommendation. That makes it easier to see what we know and what still depends on your location.

01

Scan or upload an image

Choose one JPG, PNG, or WEBP photo up to 8 MB. A bright, close view with a plain background makes it easier to distinguish a container from its surroundings. The app processes the image for this scan and does not save it.

02

AI identifies the item and material

A vision model estimates the everyday item name and its visible material. It is asked to describe only what the photo supports; it does not decide local recycling rules. When several materials look similar, the result should say so with a lower confidence level.

03

Visible condition and context are considered

The model can note visible details such as food residue, damage, or whether a container appears empty. You can also say whether you are sorting at home, in public, or at work. A photo cannot reveal everything inside an item, so hidden contents are never assumed.

04

A separate rule engine suggests the next step

Structured disposal rules match the identified item and condition to a practical action. Keeping these rules separate from image recognition makes the recommendation easier to review and update as accepted materials change.

05

You see the explanation and any uncertainty

The result includes the item, material, visible condition, recommendation, and preparation tips. If the image match is weak, the page says so clearly. Local programs differ, so the result points you back to your municipality when acceptance depends on local rules.
