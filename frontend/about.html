Smart Waste is built for the everyday moment when you pause over an item and wonder which bin is right.

01 · The idea

What is Smart Waste?

Smart Waste is a practical guide for people who want to sort things thoughtfully but do not always know what a local bin accepts. Take a photo, see what the item appears to be, and get a clear next step with the reason behind it.

It is designed to make the question easier to answer, not to make every decision for you.

02 · The need

The problem we are solving

Packaging keeps changing, collection rules can be hard to find, and a recycling symbol does not always mean an item belongs in your curbside bin. A greasy box, a clean bottle, and a loose battery all need different care.

When the answer feels unclear, useful material can be lost and the wrong thing can contaminate a collection. A helpful explanation can make the next choice less of a guess.

03 · The method

Our approach

We move from what a photo shows to what you can do next. The order matters: identify the object, consider its material, look at visible condition, take your sorting context into account, then offer guidance that explains its limits.

04 · What matters

What makes it different

The item estimate and the disposal rules are kept separate. That lets a suggestion show its reasoning instead of treating one image guess as a final answer.

We also make uncertainty visible. If the photo is unclear, the result should tell you to check before acting. It is better to be honest about a limit than to sound certain for the sake of it.

05 · Behind the scan

AI and decision technology

A vision model estimates the item, material, and visible condition in a submitted photo. A separate, readable set of rules then matches those details to guidance such as rinsing a container or taking a battery to a collection point.

Images are sent to the configured analysis service only to complete the scan and are not saved by this application. The result is general guidance; your municipality’s current instructions remain the authority for local acceptance.
